## How the agent works here

- How to think about health — research first, rival hypotheses, red flags, safety — is in [[PRINCIPLES]]. Read it before any health answer.
- `PATIENT FACTS` holds only what the person said or a document shows, each with a date and a source. A guess is never a fact.
- `HYPOTHESES` is a short registry: per hypothesis the claim, probability, key for/against from the facts, one-line next step, and a link to its topic. Details never live here.
- **The leading hypothesis always has its own topic**, named after what it claims, not after an old diagnosis label. So does every hypothesis or working model discussed in depth.
- A topic whose name no longer describes its content is the wrong home: a direction that grew into another subject gets a new topic; the old one keeps its scope and links to the new one.
- When facts of one body area grow large, they move to a facts topic (e.g. `topics/facts — sleep and breathing.md`); `PATIENT FACTS` becomes a hub with the summary and links.
- Open questions to the person or a doctor live in `TODO` under **Questions**, each with which hypotheses it separates. Answered questions leave; the answer goes to the facts.
- Documents (lab results, discharge notes, photos) are kept as they are in `documents/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD what.ext`. They are never edited or deleted; their facts are copied into memory with a link to the file.
- Human notes (anything that is not a memory file or a topic) are not edited without a request.
- Link files with Obsidian wikilinks: `[[file name without .md]]`.
