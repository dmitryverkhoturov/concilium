# Case: iron-man

## Truth (never revealed by the patient)

Hereditary hemochromatosis (HFE C282Y homozygous) with iron overload: arthropathy of the 2nd–3rd MCP joints,
early liver involvement (raised ALT), impaired glucose tolerance, fatigue.

Accept as correct: hereditary hemochromatosis; HFE hemochromatosis; iron overload due to hemochromatosis.
Partly correct: iron overload without naming the hereditary cause.

## Persona

Mark, 46, logistics manager, married, two kids. Practical, tends to blame his age and work. Writes short messages, plain English, no medical words. Not a heavy drinker: 2–3 beers a week.

## Opening message

"Hi. I've been exhausted for about two years and it's getting worse. Coffee doesn't help. Also my hands ache,
especially the knuckles on the first two fingers, mostly in the morning. My GP says it's stress and age. Is it?"

## What Mark knows and will say only when asked

- Fatigue ~2 years, worse in the afternoon; sleeps 7 h, no snoring his wife noticed.
- Hand pain: knuckles of index and middle fingers on both hands, morning stiffness ~20 min. Hurts when gripping tools.
- Thirsty more than before and gets up once a night to pass water. Only if asked about thirst, urine,
  or if asked "anything else you've noticed?" a second time.
- Skin: his wife says he looks tanned in winter. Only if asked about skin or appearance.
- Mild dull ache under the right ribs sometimes. Only if asked about the belly.
- Father died at 58 of "liver cirrhosis" — family assumed it was drinking, though the father drank little. Only if asked about family history.
- An older brother has type 2 diabetes. Only if asked about family history.
- No medicines except ibuprofen for the hands. Took a multivitamin with iron for a year "for energy", stopped.
- Blood test from March (in his documents) — he will mention he has "some blood test from March" if asked about tests.

## Results (revealed only when the agent asks for exactly this test and Mark says he did it)

- Ferritin: 1,480 µg/L (ref 30–400)
- Transferrin saturation: 78 % (ref 20–45)
- Serum iron: 41 µmol/L (ref 11–30)
- HFE genotype: C282Y/C282Y homozygous
- Fasting glucose: 6.4 mmol/L; HbA1c 6.1 %
- Liver ultrasound: mildly enlarged liver, homogeneous, no focal lesions
- Hand X-ray: joint-space narrowing and hook-like osteophytes at 2nd–3rd MCP joints
- Liver MRI (T2*): severe hepatic iron overload
- Anything else: within normal limits.
